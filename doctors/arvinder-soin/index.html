Dr. Arvinder Singh Soin

Liver Transplant Surgery · Gurugram

30 years experience

Dr. Arvinder Singh Soin is a liver transplant surgeon and Chairman of the Liver Transplantation and Hepatobiliary Surgery institute at Medanta The Medicity. He trained in transplantation at the University of Birmingham, played a central role in establishing several of India's early liver transplant programmes, and has since led one of the highest-volume living-donor liver transplant programmes in the country.

His clinical focus is living-donor liver transplantation in adults and children, deceased-donor transplant where suitable, and complex hepatobiliary surgery. He has been involved with thousands of transplant procedures across his career, with extensive experience in technically difficult cases including multi-vessel reconstruction and split-graft surgery.

He is a recipient of the Padma Shri civilian honour from the Government of India.

International patients consult him through Medanta's overseas patient services. Pre-travel imaging and report review can be arranged before the patient flies; in-person assessment, donor evaluation and surgical planning happen on arrival.
