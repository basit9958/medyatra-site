Amrita Hospital in Kochi's Edappally has operated since 1998 as a campus-style multispecialty teaching hospital affiliated with the Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham university system. It is one of the largest private hospitals in Kerala and runs high case volumes across cardiac sciences, oncology, complex transplant medicine and paediatric tertiary care.

The cardiac programme handles adult and paediatric cardiac surgery with capability across coronary intervention, complex valve work and congenital heart disease. The oncology programme runs surgical, medical and radiation oncology with stereotactic capability for selected indications. The transplant programme covers liver and kidney with a multidisciplinary team for both adult and paediatric recipients. Robotic surgery is offered across urology, gynaecology and gastrointestinal indications.

International patient coordination handles invitation letters, ground transport from Cochin International Airport (around 30 km away), accommodation matching and interpretation. Arabic interpretation is available on request for Gulf source markets.

Accommodation for accompanying family is available in serviced apartments and partner properties in Edappally and Kaloor, several within a short drive of the campus and sized for extended stays during treatment cycles.
