Robotic prostatectomy removes the prostate gland for localised prostate cancer, typically via a daVinci-robot-assisted laparoscopic approach. India has a substantial robotic urology programme across major centres with reported oncological and functional outcomes comparable to leading hospitals worldwide.

Who is it for

Prostatectomy is considered for patients with localised prostate cancer where surgery is appropriate based on stage, age and overall fitness. The decision is made together with a urologist after multidisciplinary review including the histology, imaging and broader oncology team where relevant.

What the journey looks like

Most international patients arrive three to five days before surgery for pre-operative work-up, complete the procedure with a three-day hospital stay, and remain in country for a further ten days of supervised recovery. Catheter removal usually happens around one to two weeks post-surgery; some patients prefer to remain in country for this milestone.

Cost depends on

  • Surgical approach (open versus laparoscopic versus robotic)
  • Nerve-sparing complexity
  • Hospital tier and room type
  • Pre-existing comorbidities
