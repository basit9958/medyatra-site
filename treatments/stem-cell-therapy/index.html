Stem cell therapy uses cells with regenerative potential — most commonly mesenchymal stem cells from bone marrow or adipose tissue — to support healing in selected orthopaedic, autoimmune and neurological conditions. The evidence base varies sharply by indication, and India's regulatory framework limits stem cell therapy to specific approved settings.

Who is it for

Stem cell therapy is considered for patients with an indication where the supporting evidence is meaningful and where conventional treatment has been considered and discussed. We will not facilitate treatment that does not meet a credible evidence threshold for the indication in question.

What the journey looks like

Most international patients plan a one- to two-week trip covering pre-treatment assessment, the procedure, and a short observation period. Realistic expectations are important — stem cell therapy is not a cure for most indications and outcomes are gradual where they occur at all.

Cost depends on

  • Cell source (autologous bone marrow, adipose, or umbilical cord-derived)
  • Indication and protocol complexity
  • Number of treatment cycles
  • Hospital tier and adjunctive treatments

A note on evidence

We surface this treatment with a deliberately cautious frame. Patients should understand the strength of evidence for their specific indication before travelling. Our methodology page explains how we evaluate the evidence base on every clinical surface we publish.
