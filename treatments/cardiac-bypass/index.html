Coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG) restores blood flow to the heart by routing around a blocked coronary artery using a healthy vessel taken from elsewhere in the body. India is one of the highest-volume destinations for this procedure, with multiple centres of excellence that report outcomes comparable to leading hospitals in the US and UK at a fraction of the cost.

Who is it for

CABG is typically considered for patients with significant multi-vessel coronary artery disease, left-main disease, or where less invasive options like angioplasty have not been sufficient. The decision is made together with a cardiologist after a coronary angiogram and clinical assessment.

What the journey looks like

Most international patients arrive five to seven days before surgery for pre-operative assessment, complete the procedure with a seven-day hospital stay, and remain in country for a further two weeks of supervised recovery before flying home. A structured 90-day follow-up plan with the operating team typically begins from the moment of discharge.

Cost depends on

  • Whether the surgery is on-pump or off-pump (beating heart)
  • Number of grafts required
  • Choice of hospital tier and room type
  • Pre-existing conditions affecting the recovery plan
  • Whether ICU stay extends beyond the typical post-op observation period
